**Decision: open; change the construction.** There is no general proof or counterexample here. ## What the evidence supports

- **Published:** [Mahabadi–Ramakrishnan–Tarnawski v2, §4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.29201v2) rules out universal PO + α-EFX for α>2/3. Their §5 open question is broader: whether *any* universal constant α>0 exists.
- **Source family fully characterized:** best attainable factor `2/3 + 1/K`, for every real K≥6. One integral PO allocation attains it. To improve the factor on that instance, 261,928 assignments are ruled out by one trimmed comparison and each of the remaining 216 by a fixed integral Pareto improvement. [Proof and certificate design](FAMILY.md).
- **Four-dimensional classification at each K:** the four favorite large entries can move independently through `[2K+2,∞)`. Everywhere except the source point `(3K,3K,3K,3K)`, full EFX with PO is possible. This statement holds even arbitrarily close to the source; PO can change discontinuously when an exact trade ceases to be a Pareto improvement.
- **Full-dimensional local guarantee:** changing each of the 36 entries by less than `1/3` keeps at least one efficient two-thirds-fair allocation. The [proof](NEIGHBORHOOD.md) protects the entire set of possible Pareto improvements of one anchor. An explicit example on the boundary breaks the two-allocation proof, not necessarily the guarantee. No claim of largest possible radius.

## What the research flow changed

The B06 runner hunts for *chores* using the owner's bundle; it is the wrong fairness inequality for goods. Nor is finding a *dominated* fair allocation an impossibility certificate. We used the established `litcheck` and `verify` steps, but built a goods-specific checker. First we looked for which **single allocation**, if any, actually attained the published bound. Then we used exact affine inequalities to ask whether those allocations or different ones survive on whole parameter intervals. Enumerating K values alone would not have distinguished an unattained limit from an attained endpoint. The discovery probes are not the proof. The receipt hashes the self-contained checker, which needs no numerical solver.

**Status:** local work by OpenAI Codex with configured Claude research and audit roles; unrefereed. Internal audits are not outside confirmation; this session did not compare model tiers, run a blinded trial or establish priority.

**Positive direction outside the square.** [One-surplus theorem](POSITIVE.md): under strict rankings of individual goods, full EFX and PO coexist for any n with at most n+1 goods. The proof does not require strict rankings of *bundles*. Ties between individual goods break the closure lemma, not necessarily the existence conclusion. A separate scaled-leximin condition gives two-thirds fairness for arbitrarily many goods when agents' valuations are sufficiently similar after rescaling.

**Follow-on attack.** In the [common-good and incidence search](BREAKER.md), increasing the common good from 20 to 24 while keeping the favorite large value equal to the value of three favorite medium goods (64) restored full EFX at the tested other-large value 42. A trade used by the paper now pays one agent 42 for a bundle worth 44. At K=100 the saved exact allocation list contains **252** efficient witnesses at or above the endpoint; 164 are exactly on it. We recounted the ratios as fractions and checked the proposed common bundle shape against the list: the shape is not universal. Destroying only the 16 best allocations would miss the endpoint by a wide margin. [Five exact finite rechecks](breaker/RECEIPT_E.json). These hunts do not certify a continuous common-good parameter range.

**Stopping rule met — incidence branch closed.** We changed who prizes the medium goods, classifying the entire signatures 22224, 22233 and 12333 with three favorites per person. A mixed pair that together prizes every medium good gives a fully EFX allocation at maximum total welfare. Counting the four pairs rules out any further exception except the published square in disguise. Of 4,650 labelled matrices, 4,530 have the stronger guarantee; 120 are column permutations of the square. This holds for every real K≥6, and covers every all-contested rewiring at these values with three favorites per person. [Written proof](INCIDENCE.md) · [replayable witness catalogue](INCIDENCE-RECEIPT.json). Do not extend this grid. If the negative search resumes, change the number of medium goods or the three-favorites constraint and screen for pairs covering all media before any Pareto search. The separate positive-side gap with one extra good and tied singleton values remains open here.
